What is SIL?

Support That Lives With You

Supported Independent Living (SIL) is an NDIS support that funds assistance with daily tasks in a shared or individual home — helping you live as independently as possible.

SIL is not just about the practical tasks of daily living. It's about building your capacity, developing skills and having the confidence to live the life you want — with support that adjusts as your needs change.

At Guiding Light Care Group, our SIL homes are warm, welcoming and staffed by qualified support workers with real experience in mental health and complex needs. Our Mental Health RN is always on-call to provide clinical oversight and support.

Is SIL Right for You?

Who is SIL For?

SIL is designed for NDIS participants who need daily support to live at home — but who want to maintain their independence and build their skills over time.

🧠

Psychosocial Disability

People living with the impacts of mental illness who need consistent daily support, routine building and a safe, stable environment to maintain their wellbeing.

⚡

Complex Support Needs

Participants with complex, co-occurring disability and health needs who benefit from specialist staff with clinical knowledge and responsive support.

🏡

Transitioning from Other Settings

People moving out of hospital, specialist disability accommodation, family home or other transitional housing who need supported and structured living arrangements.

Inclusions

What's Included in Our SIL?

Our SIL support is comprehensive, person-centred and designed to build your independence over time.

🍳

Daily Living Skills

Support with cooking, cleaning, laundry and personal hygiene — with a focus on building skills and independence rather than doing things for you.

💊

Medication Management

Assistance with medication administration and management, backed by our Mental Health RN's clinical oversight to ensure safe and effective use.

🤝

Social Skills & Community

Support to build and maintain relationships, engage with your community and develop the social skills that contribute to a fulfilling life.

📅

Routine & Structure

Building and maintaining healthy daily routines that support mental health, physical wellbeing and a sense of purpose and accomplishment.

🚗

Transport & Appointments

Support to attend medical appointments, therapy sessions, community activities and other commitments that are important to your goals.

🌙

Overnight & 24/7 Support

Active overnight support or sleepover arrangements depending on your assessed needs — ensuring safety and continuity of care through every hour.

Getting SIL Funded

SIL Eligibility & How to Get Started

SIL is an NDIS-funded support. Here's how to access it.

1

NDIS Plan Check

SIL must be funded in your NDIS plan under "Assistance with Daily Life." Your LAC or Support Coordinator can help confirm your eligibility.

2

SIL Assessment

An independent assessment determines your daily support hours. We can assist you to prepare for and understand this process.

3

Home Matching

We match you to a home that suits your needs, preferences and compatibility with any existing housemates — getting the right fit matters.

4

Move In & Settle

A supported transition plan ensures your move is smooth, your support team is introduced properly, and your new home feels like home from day one.

Common Questions

SIL Frequently Asked Questions

SIL (Supported Independent Living) funds the support you receive at home — the support workers, their time and the assistance they provide. SDA (Specialist Disability Accommodation) funds the building — the physical property itself. You can have SIL in an SDA property, in a standard rental, or in your own home. Not all SIL participants need SDA.
We take compatibility very seriously. Before placing a participant in a shared home, we consult with all existing residents and their support networks. We consider personality, routines, support needs and lifestyle preferences to ensure a good match. You always have the right to say no to a proposed housemate arrangement.
Your SIL funding and support hours are reviewed regularly as part of your NDIS plan cycle. If your needs change significantly between reviews, we can assist you to request an unscheduled plan review. We work with you and your support coordinator to ensure your SIL always reflects your current needs.
Our SIL homes have detailed crisis response plans for every participant. Our Mental Health RN is available on-call at all times. In a mental health crisis, our support workers follow evidence-based protocols, including contacting our RN, engaging mental health crisis teams when appropriate, and ensuring the participant's safety and dignity throughout.

SIL is funded under the "Assistance with Daily Life" support category (Core Supports). Unlike most other NDIS supports, SIL funding is quoted by the provider based on an individual assessment of your support needs — rather than being a set line item in your plan. This means your SIL funding reflects your actual needs. We can assist you to understand the quoting process.
