Your NDIS Navigator

Turning Your Plan into Action

Support Coordinators help you understand and implement your NDIS plan — cutting through the complexity to ensure your supports are working together effectively and you're getting the most from your funding.

The NDIS can feel overwhelming, especially at first. There are providers to find, service agreements to manage, budgets to track and reviews to prepare for. Your Support Coordinator takes on this complexity so you can focus on living your life.

Our coordinators have deep knowledge of the Hunter region's service landscape and specialist expertise in psychosocial disability — meaning we know exactly who to call, what to request, and how to build the right team around you.

Two Levels of Support

Support Coordination vs Specialist Support Coordination

The NDIS funds two levels of support coordination. Here's what each includes.

📋

Support Coordination

Standard support coordination helps you understand your plan, find and connect with providers, develop service agreements, monitor your budget and prepare for plan reviews. Ideal for participants with moderate complexity who need guidance and coordination but not intensive specialist input.

  • ✓Plan implementation and provider connections
  • ✓Service agreement development
  • ✓Budget monitoring and reporting
  • ✓Plan review preparation
⭐

Specialist Support Coordination

For participants with complex needs — including complex psychosocial disability, high risk situations or multiple provider coordination challenges. Specialist Support Coordination requires a higher level of skill and clinical knowledge to navigate difficult, high-stakes support environments.

  • ✓All Standard SC inclusions, plus:
  • ✓Complex needs assessment
  • ✓Crisis intervention coordination
  • ✓Multi-agency collaboration
  • ✓Clinical oversight integration

Common Questions

Support Coordination FAQ

Support Coordination is funded from your NDIS plan's Capacity Building budget — specifically the "Support Coordination" line item. It does not come from your Core Supports budget. If you have Support Coordination funding in your plan, there is no out-of-pocket cost to you for this service.
The NDIS has conflict of interest provisions around this. While a Support Coordinator can work for the same organisation as your other providers, they must manage any conflict of interest transparently. We are open with all participants about our full service offering and always prioritise your interests — including connecting you with other providers when that's the best outcome for you.
Not all NDIS plans include Support Coordination — it depends on your circumstances and complexity of needs. If you don't have it but feel you need it, you can request it be included at your next plan review. Your LAC can help with this. Alternatively, Local Area Coordinators (LACs) provide some free coordination support for all participants.
Specialist Support Coordination is for participants with complex situations — such as those with significant mental health needs, those at risk of harm, those transitioning from hospital or justice settings, or those with multiple agencies involved in their support. If you're dealing with a complex situation and feel standard SC isn't meeting your needs, talk to your NDIS planner about upgrading to Specialist Support Coordination.
