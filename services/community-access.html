What We Offer

Your World, Wider Than You Know

Community Access support helps NDIS participants engage meaningfully with their community — pursuing social connections, recreational interests, employment goals and everything in between.

For people with psychosocial disability, getting out into the community can feel overwhelming. Our support workers are trained to provide the right level of support — not doing it for you, but walking beside you as you build the confidence and skills to connect.

Whether it's your first trip to a community garden, a regular catch-up with a community sports group, or supported transport to your GP, our Community Access team makes it happen — safely, consistently and in a way that builds your long-term independence.

  • Individually matched support workers
  • Goal-driven activity planning
  • Flexible scheduling to suit your routine
  • Transport assistance included
What We Can Support

Activities We Support

Community Access is broad — these are some of the many things we can support you to do.

🎭

Social & Recreational

Attending social events, joining hobby groups, going to the cinema, sports clubs, art classes, music events and other recreational activities that bring you joy and connection.

💼

Employment & Volunteering

Supported travel to work, volunteering opportunities, work experience programs and activities that build your employment-related skills and confidence.

📚

Education & Training

Attending TAFE, adult education courses, community workshops, literacy programs and any learning activity that supports your personal development goals.

🏥

Health Appointments

Transport and support to attend medical, dental, therapy and other health appointments — including preparation for appointments and follow-up support.

🌱

Skill Building

Learning to use public transport independently, grocery shopping, budgeting, cooking classes and other practical life skills that increase your everyday independence.

🧘

Wellness & Fitness

Attending the gym, swimming, walking groups, yoga classes and other physical wellness activities that support your mental and physical health.

The Process

How Community Access Works

1

Goal Setting

We start with what you want to achieve — whether that's making new friends, building employment skills or simply getting out and enjoying your community more.

2

Worker Matching

We match you with a support worker who shares your interests, understands your needs and will genuinely support your community goals.

3

Activity Planning

Together we build a schedule of activities aligned to your goals — structured enough to be effective, flexible enough to be enjoyable.

4

Review & Grow

Regular check-ins ensure your community access supports continue to meet your evolving goals — gradually increasing independence as your confidence grows.
