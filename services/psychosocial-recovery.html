Psychosocial Recovery Coaching

Specialist support for your mental health recovery journey — building resilience, reclaiming purpose and navigating your NDIS plan with confidence.

Mental Health Focus

Your Recovery, Your Way

Psychosocial Recovery Coaching (PRC) is a specialised NDIS support designed specifically for people whose disability arises from mental illness. Your Recovery Coach works alongside you — not above you — as a skilled, knowledgeable partner in your recovery journey.

Unlike support coordination, PRC has a distinctly mental health recovery focus. Your coach understands the fluctuating nature of psychosocial disability, the importance of building long-term capacity, and how to connect your recovery goals to your NDIS plan in meaningful ways.

Our Recovery Coaches are people with lived experience of mental health challenges and formal training in recovery-oriented practice — bringing both professional knowledge and genuine understanding to every conversation.

Your Coach's Role

What Does a Recovery Coach Do?

A Recovery Coach wears many hats — but always keeps your recovery goals at the centre of everything.

🗺️

Navigate Your NDIS Plan

Help you understand what your plan funds, how to use your budget effectively, and which supports will best help you achieve your mental health recovery goals.

🎯

Goal Setting & Planning

Work with you to set meaningful, achievable recovery goals — translating your hopes and aspirations into a practical plan of action that honours your pace and values.

🔗

Connect You to Services

Build and maintain connections to mental health services, community supports, allied health providers and peer support networks that support your recovery.

💪

Build Capacity

Develop your skills, confidence and resilience — with the goal of needing less support over time as you build your own capacity to manage your wellbeing.

🚨

Crisis Prevention & Response

Help you develop and implement a personal crisis plan — identifying early warning signs, coping strategies and the right supports to prevent and manage mental health crises.

👥

Coordinate Your Team

Work collaboratively with your broader support team — including psychiatrists, GPs, support workers and family — to ensure a consistent, coordinated approach to your recovery.

🌱
Our Approach

Recovery-Oriented Practice

Recovery is not about "getting back to normal" — it's about building a meaningful, satisfying and hopeful life, even in the presence of mental health challenges.

Our Recovery Coaches are trained in and committed to recovery-oriented practice — an approach that emphasises hope, personal agency, social connection and a focus on strengths rather than deficits.

We recognise that recovery is not linear. There will be good days and hard days. Our coaches meet you wherever you are, without judgment, and help you build the tools to navigate the hard days and celebrate the good ones.

  • Hope as a foundation — recovery is always possible
  • Strengths-based — building on what you have
  • Self-determination — your life, your decisions
  • Peer connection — lived experience as expertise
  • Holistic — the whole person, not just the diagnosis
Eligibility

Who Qualifies for Psychosocial Recovery Coaching?

PRC is specifically designed for NDIS participants with a primary psychosocial disability.

🧠

Primary Psychosocial Disability

Your NDIS eligibility is based primarily on a mental health condition such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, major depressive disorder, PTSD or another persistent mental illness.

📋

PRC in Your NDIS Plan

Psychosocial Recovery Coaching must be included in your NDIS plan. It is funded under the Capacity Building support category as "Improved Daily Living."

💡

Not Sure? We Can Help

If you're unsure whether you have PRC funding, or want to request it at your next plan review, our team can help you understand your options and build the case to your planner.
