What is ILO?

A New Way to Think About Living

Individualised Living Options (ILO) is an NDIS support that funds the exploration and set-up of living arrangements tailored entirely to you — going beyond the traditional SIL model to find truly creative solutions.

ILO recognises that not everyone's housing and support needs fit a standard mould. Some participants want to live with a chosen person or family. Others want a supported housemate arrangement. ILO funds the thinking, planning and set-up of whatever works best for you.

At Guiding Light Care Group, we guide you through the entire ILO exploration and implementation process — from initial conversations about what you want, to matching, trialling and establishing long-term arrangements.

Living Arrangement Types

ILO Arrangement Options

ILO can take many forms. Here are the three most common arrangement types we support.

🏡

Host Arrangement

You live with a carefully matched, vetted host family or individual who provides support as part of their daily life. This creates a natural, home-like environment with genuine personal connection and community inclusion.

  • ✓Warm, family environment
  • ✓Natural community inclusion
  • ✓Vetted and supported hosts
🏠

Co-Residency

A support worker or carer lives in the same home as you, providing consistent, familiar support in a shared living arrangement. This is ideal for people who value continuity and knowing who their support person is.

  • ✓Consistent, familiar support
  • ✓Shared living, maintained privacy
  • ✓Flexible support levels
👥

Supported Housemate

You live with a housemate who receives a contribution towards their rent or living costs in exchange for providing informal supports. This creates a peer-like, natural relationship and reduces social isolation.

  • ✓Peer-based relationship
  • ✓Reduced social isolation
  • ✓Genuine community connection
The ILO Journey

The ILO Exploration Process

ILO starts with exploration — funded time to think, plan and trial what living arrangements could work best for you.

1

Exploration Funding

Your NDIS plan includes ILO exploration funding. We use this time to have deep, supported conversations about your housing dreams, values and preferences.

2

Options Design

Together we design a range of ILO options that could work for you — considering your support needs, relationships, community ties and long-term goals.

3

Matching & Trialling

We match you with potential hosts, housemates or co-residency candidates and support a structured trial period to ensure the arrangement is right for everyone.

4

Implementation

Once a great match is found, we formalise the arrangement, provide ongoing support to all parties, and review regularly to ensure it continues to work well.

Questions & Answers

ILO Frequently Asked Questions

SIL (Supported Independent Living) is a more structured model where you receive support in a provider-managed home, typically with other participants. ILO is more flexible and creative — it funds the design and establishment of personalised living arrangements, including with chosen people, family-style settings or community-based options. ILO often results in more natural, community-connected living arrangements.
The exploration phase typically takes 3–6 months, depending on your circumstances and the complexity of your preferred arrangements. NDIS funds this exploration time separately. Once a suitable arrangement is identified, the implementation phase can take a further 2–4 months including matching, trialling and formalising the arrangement.
ILO can sometimes involve family members in specific circumstances — for example, through a supported housemate arrangement with a sibling. However, there are NDIS rules about funding supports delivered by immediate family members. Our team can work through the specific options available for your situation during the exploration phase.
We build robust support and risk management plans for every ILO arrangement. If an arrangement does break down, we have contingency plans in place and work quickly to find an interim solution. No participant is ever left without safe housing as a result of an ILO arrangement ending — we ensure transitional support is in place.
ILO appears in your NDIS plan under "Assistance with Daily Life" (Core Supports) as "ILO — Exploration and Design" and/or "ILO — Support in a Living Arrangement." If you don't have ILO in your current plan but you're interested, speak to your support coordinator or LAC about requesting it at your next plan review. We can help you build the case for ILO funding.
