Evidence-Based Care

Therapeutic Support That Makes a Real Difference

Therapeutic Supports under the NDIS fund a range of assessments, therapies and skill development activities delivered by qualified allied health professionals to help you develop independence and achieve your goals.

Unlike general support, therapeutic supports are targeted, structured and outcomes-focused — designed to build specific skills, address functional limitations and support you to participate more fully in life.

At Guiding Light Care Group, we coordinate access to a range of allied health professionals and therapeutic approaches, always ensuring they are trauma-informed, person-centred and genuinely aligned with your goals.

Therapeutic Approaches

Types of Therapy We Support

We coordinate access to a range of NDIS-funded therapeutic supports to address your specific goals and needs.

🧩

Occupational Therapy

OT supports you to develop everyday living skills, improve home and community function, and assess assistive technology and home modification needs.

💬

Psychology & Counselling

Evidence-based psychological interventions including CBT, DBT, ACT and trauma-focused therapies to address mental health, emotional regulation and behavioural challenges.

🗣️

Speech Pathology

Communication assessments and therapy to support speech, language, social communication, and the development of AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) where needed.

🏃

Exercise Physiology

Structured exercise programs designed to improve physical function, mental health, pain management and overall wellbeing for people with disability.

🎵

Music & Art Therapy

Creative therapies that use artistic expression to support emotional regulation, communication, self-esteem and social connection — particularly effective for people with psychosocial disability.

🍎

Dietetics & Nutrition

Dietary assessment and support to address health conditions, weight management, eating difficulties and the nutritional impact of medications commonly used in mental health treatment.

Coordinated Care

Allied Health Coordination

Getting the right allied health professionals in the right order, working from the same goal framework, is critical to therapeutic outcomes. That's where we come in.

We work with your Support Coordinator to identify the therapeutic supports you need, help you find qualified providers within your budget, and ensure all your allied health professionals are working as a coordinated team rather than in isolation.

We also provide therapeutic support coordination — ensuring that recommendations from your allied health team are implemented consistently across all your supports, including in your SIL or ILO home if applicable.

  • Provider vetting and recommendation
  • Goal-aligned therapeutic planning
  • Cross-team communication and coordination
  • Implementation support across all services
Our Foundation

Trauma-Informed Practice

All our therapeutic supports are grounded in trauma-informed principles — because for many people with psychosocial disability, trauma is central to their experience.

🛡️

Safety First

We prioritise physical, emotional and psychological safety in every therapeutic interaction — creating an environment where healing and growth are possible.

🤲

Trustworthiness

Transparent communication, consistent follow-through and honest relationships build the trust that effective therapeutic work requires — especially for trauma survivors.

💪

Empowerment

We focus on building your strengths and agency — not managing your deficits. Every therapeutic goal is chosen by you, for you, at your pace.

Therapeutic Supports FAQ

Therapeutic Supports are funded under the Capacity Building support category, specifically "Improved Daily Living." This funding is separate from your Core Supports and is intended to build your long-term capacity and independence. The specific therapies funded depend on your goals, functional assessment and what the NDIS considers "reasonable and necessary."
Yes — you have the right to choose your own therapist or allied health provider. We can provide recommendations and help you find a good fit, but the final choice is always yours. What's important is finding someone you feel safe with and who has appropriate expertise in your area of need.
Therapy is delivered by qualified allied health professionals (psychologists, OTs, speech pathologists, etc.) and has a structured, outcomes-focused clinical framework. Support work focuses more on practical assistance and daily living. Both are valuable — and when they work together with aligned goals, the outcomes for participants are significantly better.
Yes — many allied health professionals can deliver services in your home, in the community, or via telehealth. For people with psychosocial disability who have difficulty attending clinic appointments, home-based or telehealth delivery can be a much more accessible and effective option.
For NDIS-funded therapeutic supports, you generally don't need a medical referral — you just need the relevant funding in your NDIS plan. Some specific services (like psychology) may require an initial assessment. Your Support Coordinator or our team can help you navigate the referral process if needed.
