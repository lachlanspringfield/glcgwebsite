About Guiding Light Care Group

We're a registered NDIS provider rooted in the Hunter region, built on genuine relationships, clinical expertise and an unwavering belief in every person's right to choose how they live.

🌿
Our Story

Born from a Belief That Everyone Deserves Better

Guiding Light Care Group was founded by a team of clinicians and support workers who saw a gap in the NDIS market — providers who truly understood psychosocial disability and could deliver both clinical rigour and genuine human warmth.

After years of working within the sector and witnessing participants fall through the cracks — particularly those with complex mental health needs — our founders decided to build something different. A provider that never loses sight of the person behind the plan.

Today, we support over 100 participants across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and Maitland, delivering SIL, ILO, Community Access, Psychosocial Recovery Coaching, Support Coordination and Therapeutic Supports with the same passion and integrity we started with.

Why We Started

We've Seen the Gap. We're Closing It.

Too many NDIS providers lack the clinical knowledge to safely support people with psychosocial disability. Too many participants cycle through services that don't understand their mental health needs.

We started Guiding Light Care Group to change that. We built a team with real mental health experience — including registered nurse oversight — and wrapped it in genuine, recovery-oriented practice.

Our model means that when a participant experiences a mental health crisis at 2am, they're not just calling a support worker reading from a script. They're connected to a team with the clinical skills to respond safely and appropriately.

  • Founders with lived and professional mental health experience
  • On-call Mental Health RN available around the clock
  • Crisis response protocols embedded into every service
  • Close relationships with Hunter mental health services
How We Work

Our Approach to Support

Three guiding principles shape every interaction, every plan and every outcome we work towards.

🛡️

Trauma-Informed Practice

We recognise the profound impact of trauma on a person's life. Every interaction is designed to build safety, trust and choice — never to re-traumatise or overwhelm.

👤

Person-Centred Design

Support plans are co-designed with participants — not for them. You lead the conversation about what you want your life to look like, and we build the supports around that vision.

🏥

Clinical Oversight

Our on-call Mental Health Registered Nurse provides clinical governance across all our services. This means expert oversight, safe medication management and qualified crisis response — always.
