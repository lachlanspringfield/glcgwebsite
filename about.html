Our Story

About Guiding Light Care Group

We're a registered NDIS provider rooted in the Hunter region, built on genuine relationships, clinical expertise and an unwavering belief in every person's right to choose how they live.

🌿
Our Story

Born from a Belief That Everyone Deserves Better

Guiding Light Care Group was founded by Lochie Springfield — a Registered Nurse, Behaviour Support Practitioner and mental health specialist who saw a gap: providers who truly understood psychosocial disability and could deliver clinical rigour alongside genuine human warmth.

Lochie's motto is simple: "Recovery through relationships." After years in Emergency, Mental Health and Aged Care Nursing, and witnessing participants fall through the cracks of services that didn't understand their needs, he built something different — a provider that never loses sight of the person behind the plan.

Today, our growing team supports participants across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and Maitland, delivering SIL, ILO, Community Access, Psychosocial Recovery Coaching, Support Coordination and Therapeutic Supports with the same passion and integrity we started with.

Why We Started

We've Seen the Gap. We're Closing It.

Australian community care doesn't need another provider that simply fits the existing mould. It requires innovation to meet the evolving needs of our diverse communities.

Our support workers are closely trained and supported by postgraduate-level Registered Nurses and Behaviour Support Practitioners. This level of governance ensures participants receive safe, responsive care — and gives participants and their support networks confidence that complex needs get met properly from day one.

When a participant experiences a mental health challenge, they're not just calling a support worker reading from a script. They're connected to a clinically informed team built to respond safely and compassionately.

  • Nurse-led governance — postgrad RN and BSP oversight
  • Clinical training embedded into all support worker practice
  • Crisis response protocols built into every service
  • Close relationships with Hunter mental health services
How We Work

Our Approach to Support

Three guiding principles shape every interaction, every plan and every outcome we work towards.

🛡️

Trauma-Informed Practice

We recognise the profound impact of trauma on a person's life. Every interaction is designed to build safety, trust and choice — never to re-traumatise or overwhelm.

👤

Person-Centred Design

Support plans are co-designed with participants — not for them. You lead the conversation about what you want your life to look like, and we build the supports around that vision.

🏥

Clinical Oversight

Our on-call Mental Health Registered Nurse provides clinical governance across all our services. This means expert oversight, safe medication management and qualified crisis response — always.
