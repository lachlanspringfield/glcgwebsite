NDIS Funding Guide

Everything you need to know about the NDIS, how it's funded and how to access our services — explained simply and clearly.

📋
The Basics

What is the NDIS?

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) is an Australian Government program that provides funding to people under 65 with a permanent and significant disability — to help them access the supports they need to live the life they choose.

The NDIS is managed by the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA). It is not welfare — it is individualised funding based on your disability-related support needs and the goals you want to achieve.

Once approved, you receive an NDIS plan — a document that outlines your goals and the funding allocated to achieve them. You can then use this funding to access supports from registered providers like Guiding Light Care Group.

  • Available to eligible Australians under 65
  • Based on permanent and significant disability
  • Individualised funding for your specific goals
  • Reviewed regularly as your needs change
Understanding Your Plan

How NDIS Plans Work

Your NDIS plan is divided into three budget categories. Here's what each one funds.

⚙️

Core Supports

Funding for everyday activities and supports — including daily personal activities, transport, consumables and social and community participation. This is where SIL, ILO and Community Access are funded.

📈

Capacity Building

Funding to build your skills and independence over time — including support coordination, psychosocial recovery coaching, therapeutic supports, employment and improved daily living.

🏗️

Capital Supports

Funding for higher-cost items and home modifications — including assistive technology, home modification assessments and Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA) if eligible.

We Accept All Funding Types

Plan Management Options We Work With

No matter how your NDIS plan is managed, we can work with you.

🏛️

Agency Managed

The NDIA pays providers directly from your plan. You can only use registered providers. As a registered NDIS provider, Guiding Light Care Group can work with Agency Managed participants.

📊

Plan Managed

A plan manager handles your invoices and claims. You can use both registered and unregistered providers, giving you more flexibility. We work with all plan management companies in the Hunter region.

👤

Self Managed

You manage your own budget and can use any provider. You pay invoices directly and claim reimbursement from the NDIS portal. We support self-managed participants and provide clear, compliant invoicing.

Frequently Asked Questions

NDIS Funding FAQ

To be eligible for the NDIS you must: be under 65 years old when you first apply; be an Australian citizen, permanent resident or holder of a Protected Special Category Visa; and have a permanent disability that significantly affects your ability to take part in everyday activities. You can check your eligibility on the NDIS website (ndis.gov.au) or contact the NDIS on 1800 800 110.
If you're not yet on the NDIS, we can still help you understand the process and, in some cases, assist you to prepare your application. Contact us and our team will discuss your situation and point you in the right direction — including connecting you with your local NDIS LAC (Local Area Coordinator).
"Reasonable and necessary" is the NDIS's test for what supports it will fund. A support must be related to your disability, not something that would typically be provided by family or funded by another system (like health or education), represent value for money, and help you pursue your goals or build independence. Our team can help you understand what is and isn't likely to be funded in your situation.
Yes — you have the right to change providers at any time. Your service agreement will specify the notice period required. Generally, 2–4 weeks notice is standard. Changing providers does not affect your NDIS plan or funding — your funding stays with you, not your provider.
The NDIS sets maximum prices (Price Limits) for most support categories through its Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits document, updated annually. Providers cannot charge more than these limits. We always charge within NDIS Price Limits and are transparent about our rates in our service agreements. We can provide you with our current rate schedule on request.
NDIS plans are reviewed periodically — typically every 12 months, but sometimes more or less frequently. At a plan review, your goals, support needs and funding are reassessed. It's an opportunity to increase, decrease or change your funding. We support our participants and their coordinators to prepare for plan reviews with progress evidence and updated support needs documentation.
