About Us

We're More Than a Support Provider — We're a Community

Founded on the belief that every person deserves to live the life they choose, Guiding Light Care Group was built by people who understand the complexity of psychosocial disability firsthand.

We combine clinical expertise — with on-call Mental Health Registered Nurse oversight — with genuine human connection. Our team walks alongside participants, families and carers, building capacity and celebrating every step towards independence.

  • Trauma-informed, person-centred practice
  • Clinical mental health oversight on-call 24/7
  • Culturally responsive and inclusive support
  • Transparent, honest and consistent care
  • Recovery-oriented approach to independence

Our Approach

Our Values

Six principles that shape every interaction, every plan and every life we're privileged to support.

Be Genuine

We lead with honesty, warmth and transparency. Our team is made up of naturally compassionate people who value real, respectful connection.

Empower People

We take a strengths-based, person-centred approach that puts participants in control of their journey — helping them grow and achieve their goals.

Trust

We show up with integrity, consistency and care. Our participants trust us to provide reliable, high-quality support that respects their needs and wellbeing.

Excellence

We are committed to delivering high-quality, evidence-based support that continues to evolve through ongoing training, learning and reflection.

Adaptability

We understand that needs and goals change. Our team stays flexible and responsive, adjusting support to suit each individual while remaining consistent.

Connection

Strong relationships are at the heart of great support. We help participants feel supported, understood and connected to the people and community around them.
