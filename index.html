About Us

We're More Than a Support Provider — We're a Community

Founded on the belief that every person deserves to live the life they choose, Guiding Light Care Group was built by people who understand the complexity of psychosocial disability firsthand.

We combine clinical expertise — with on-call Mental Health Registered Nurse oversight — with genuine human connection. Our team walks alongside participants, families and carers, building capacity and celebrating every step towards independence.

  • Trauma-informed, person-centred practice
  • Clinical mental health oversight on-call 24/7
  • Culturally responsive and inclusive support
  • Transparent, honest and consistent care
  • Recovery-oriented approach to independence

Getting Started

How It Works

Starting your journey with us is simple. We take the complexity out of navigating supports so you can focus on what matters.

1

Reach Out

Contact us by phone, email or through our enquiry form. Our friendly team will respond within one business day.

2

Discovery Meeting

We arrange a no-obligation discovery conversation — learning about your goals, your NDIS plan and what's important to you.

3

Personalised Plan

Together we build your individual support plan, connecting you to the right services, staff and housing options.

4

Begin Your Journey

Supports begin with an ongoing commitment to review, refine and celebrate your progress every step of the way.
