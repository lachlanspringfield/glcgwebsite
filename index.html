Who We Are

A Care Team That Goes Beyond the Plan

Guiding Light Care Group is a registered NDIS provider and leader in accommodation, community access and therapeutic supports across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie & Maitland.

We believe every person deserves to live with dignity, choice and purpose. Our team combines clinical expertise — including on-call Mental Health RN oversight — with genuine, person-centred care that adapts to each participant's unique goals and circumstances.

We specialise in supporting people with psychosocial disability, working alongside participants, families, and allied health professionals to build the foundations for a truly independent life.

  • ✦Empowerment — Supporting your goals, your way
  • ✦Integrity — Honest, transparent and accountable
  • ✦Innovation — Pioneering new approaches to care
  • ✦Compassion — Genuine relationships at the heart of everything

What We Offer

Our NDIS Supports

We offer a range of supports designed to help participants live independently, engage with their community and reach their personal goals.

🏡

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

24/7 support in a comfortable shared or individual home environment. Our SIL services are tailored to help participants build daily living skills, maintain routines and thrive in a safe, supportive setting.

  • Personalised support plans
  • Skill-building focus
  • On-call Mental Health RN

Individualised Living Options (ILO)

Flexible, creative home and living arrangements designed around you. ILO explores innovative housing and support options beyond traditional models — from host arrangements to co-residency with supported housemates.

  • Co-design with the participant
  • Flexible arrangements
  • Long-term stability focus
Find Out More →
🌍

Community Access

Supporting participants to engage meaningfully with their community — whether attending social activities, appointments, recreational outings or pursuing employment and education goals.

  • Social & recreational activities
  • Transport assistance
  • Employment & education support

🧠

Psychosocial Recovery Coaching

Specialised coaching for participants living with mental health challenges. Our coaches work alongside participants to build resilience, develop coping strategies and work towards their recovery and independence goals.

  • Mental health expertise
  • Goal-oriented recovery plans
  • Holistic wellbeing approach

Support Coordination

Helping you navigate the NDIS and make the most of your plan. Our coordinators connect you with the right services, manage provider relationships and ensure your supports are working together effectively.

Getting Started

How It Works

Getting started with Guiding Light Care is simple. We walk with you every step of the way.

01

Reach Out

Contact us by phone or email. Tell us a little about yourself and what you're looking for. There's no pressure — just a friendly conversation.

→
02

Free Consultation

We meet with you (and your support network if you'd like) to understand your goals, needs and NDIS plan. We'll explain exactly how we can help.

→
03

Your Support Plan

Together, we co-design a support plan built around your unique goals — flexible, person-centred and reviewed regularly as your needs evolve.

→
04

Begin Your Journey

Your dedicated support team begins working with you. We stay by your side, adjusting as needed to ensure you're always moving toward your goals.
